Its code, item textures and data are original (made for CobbleEffort by ellecat5112 and contributors). The fusion
rules follow the fan game Pokémon Infinite Fusion's (head and body, stat weighting, types); no art or code from that
game is included. Fused Pokémon are drawn from Cobblemon's own models at runtime.

Fusion colours: for pairs with a custom sprite in Pokémon Infinite Fusion, the colours its artist chose were read from
the sprite (as published on the Infinite Dex, infinitefusiondex.com) and are applied to Cobblemon's textures. Only
colour values, the game's fusion names and the artists' credits are included, never the sprites. The artists are
credited per fusion in game and all together in CREDITS.txt. Not affiliated with Nintendo,
Game Freak, The Pokémon Company, the Cobblemon team or the Infinite Fusion team.
