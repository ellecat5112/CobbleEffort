CobbleEffort: Fusion @VERSION@ (Minecraft 1.21.1, Cobblemon 1.8.4; Fabric and NeoForge)
==================================================================================

DNA Splicers for Cobblemon, after the fan game Pokémon Infinite Fusion: fuse any two of your Pokémon into one.

Install: put the jar for your loader in your mods folder (and on the server). Needs Cobblemon 1.8.4+ and
Architectury API. Works on its own; it also fits with the other CobbleEffort mods.

HOW TO FUSE
  Right-click with DNA Splicers. Choose the HEAD, then the BODY (both from your party). The head leaves your party
  and the body becomes the fusion. Not during a battle.
  - DNA Splicers: the fusion is the average of the two levels. Used up by fusing.
  - Super Splicers: the fusion takes the higher of the two levels.
  - Infinite Splicers: like DNA Splicers, never used up.
  - DNA Reversers: choose a fusion and its head and body swap places. Used up.
  To unfuse, use any splicers and choose the fusion: both Pokémon come back at the fusion's level (the head goes to
  your PC if your party is full). Unfusing doesn't use the splicers up.

HEAD AND BODY (Infinite Fusion's rules)
  - Base stats: the head gives two thirds of HP, Sp. Atk and Sp. Def; the body two thirds of Attack, Defense and
    Speed; the other third comes from the other Pokémon.
  - Types: the head's first type, then the body's second type (or its only type). A repeated type is replaced by the
    body's other type; a Normal/Flying head gives Flying.
  - Ability: the body's to start with. A clickable choice in chat lets you take the head's instead (or type
    /fusion ability). Moves: the fusion keeps the body's moves, and can relearn the head's.
  - The fusion keeps the body's nature, IVs, EVs and gender, and gets a fusion name (Bulbasaur + Charmander =
    Bulbander) unless it has a nickname.
  - It battles with the fused stats and types, and its summary shows them. /fusion info lists your fusions.

THE LOOK
  Every pair works automatically: the fusion is the body's model with the head species' head (with its eyes, ears,
  horns and hair) on its neck, sized to fit, in the head's own colours (shiny or regional too). The head turns and
  moves with the body. Hand-made fusion forms for favourite pairs may come later.

CRAFTING
  DNA Splicers: amethyst shard over iron ingot, redstone, iron ingot, over iron ingot.
  Super Splicers: diamond over gold ingot, DNA Splicers, gold ingot, over gold ingot.
  Infinite Splicers: nether star over echo shard, Super Splicers, echo shard, over echo shard.
  DNA Reversers: lapis, quartz, lapis over redstone.
