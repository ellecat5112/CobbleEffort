CobbleEffort: Digital @VERSION@ (Minecraft 1.21.1, Cobblemon 1.8.4)
=====================================================================

A Digimon crossover for Cobblemon. Since 0.2.0 the Digimon themselves live here (they used to ship inside
CobbleEffort; their ids are unchanged, so Digimon you already caught stay as they are). They spawn, battle, level up
and evolve exactly like Pokémon, on hand-built models, and are added in wikimon.net's alphabetical order a batch at a
time; wikimon decides who evolves into whom (when several evolutions are possible at once, Cobblemon offers them all
and you pick). Sizes: Adult 15 blocks tall, Perfect 25, Ultimate 35, Super Ultimate 45.

* Digiball: a Poké Ball made for Digimon, three times as good as a Poké Ball on any Digimon (and an ordinary
  Poké Ball on anything else). Craft one from a Poké Ball, redstone, lapis lazuli and a copper ingot (shapeless), or
  four from a Great Ball ringed with redstone, copper ingots and lapis lazuli.
* Digitama (Digi-Egg): keep one in your inventory and it warms up (the bar under it fills over 5 minutes). When it
  starts to shine, use it to hatch a baby Digimon at level 1, straight into your party (or your PC if the party is
  full). For now every Digitama hatches a Koromon. Find them in dungeon, mineshaft, desert and jungle temple,
  ancient city and village chests, and wild Digimon leave one behind 5% of the times you defeat them in battle.

Install: Fabric or NeoForge with Architectury API and Cobblemon 1.8.4. Shared as a main jar plus texture jars
("CobbleEffort: Digital Textures N"): install all of them.
